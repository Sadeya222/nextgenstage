      Why NexGen

      UK manufactured portable staging, handcrafted in Sheffield

      NexGen Portable Staging & Dancefloors — fast, eco-friendly, UK-made magnetic portable staging. Lighter, faster, safer and longer lasting than traditional lock-based systems.

      1

      Totally magnetic — no locks, no keys, nothing to replace

      NexGen platforms are totally magnetic — no locks and no keys required, which means less hassle and more security. Other branded platforms such as Revostage, Intellistage, Alustage and Go-Pak can have up to 8 locks that need replacing over time. With NexGen there is nothing to replace, so platforms stay 100% functional for their whole lifespan.

      2

      Over twenty years of UK stage manufacturing experience

      We manufacture our products in the UK — handcrafted in Sheffield — and deliver them directly to you, so you get the best quality at the best price. With over twenty years of stage manufacturing experience, NexGen is a trusted UK manufacturer of portable staging and dancefloors.

      3

      Eco-friendly, sustainable materials

      The Platinum range uses recycled core material, sustainably sourced plywood, recycled carpet and less hazardous adhesives — which means less waste and more responsibility, with a smaller carbon footprint than aluminium-based systems.

      4

      ECAL funding — up to 20% off for eligible organisations

      The Education, Charity, Arts & Leisure (ECAL) Funding programme offers eligible organisations discounts of up to 20% on staging equipment — with no complex application forms, guaranteed funding for eligible organisations, and savings that apply to complete staging systems and accessories.

      Build your own perfect-sized portable stage or dancefloor

      Using our deck platforms and riser legs, you can build your own perfect-sized portable stage or dancefloor. Storage trolleys, handrails, ramps, backdrops and a full range of parts are in stock in Sheffield too.

      The Education, Charity, Arts & Leisure (ECAL) Funding programme offers eligible organisations discounts of up to 20% on staging equipment purchases. It is spearheaded by NexGen Staging Limited and applies to complete staging systems and accessories, with no complex application forms or lengthy waiting periods. To get started, use the online quotation tool at and select “”, or call 0114 399 2978.

      NexGen riser legs are available in fixed heights of 200mm, 300mm, 400mm, 600mm, 800mm and 1000mm. Every SteelCity module is a deck & riser combination, so you can choose any surface and any height combo — package pricing stays the same across heights.

      Platforms are light but strong: around 7.2–7.8kg for a Platinum 1×1m platform and 8.5kg for a SteelCity 1×1m module. Load weight is up to 1,500kg per platform, and a NexGen deck with NexGen riser legs can take up to 2+ tons depending on the height — always check the individual height specification.

      Yes — NexGen staging is totally magnetic, with no locks and no keys. Other branded platforms such as Revostage, Intellistage, Alustage and Go-Pak can have up to 8 locks that need replacing over time. With NexGen there is nothing to replace, which is why Platinum platforms last up to 15 years and stay 100% functional throughout their lifespan.

      NexGen platforms are available in carpet — including grey and anthracite rib carpet — or a tough black ABS (HardBlack) non-slip finish. Choir packages are supplied in grey carpet, and fascia is available in cloth valance or hard fascia to any depth.

      We manufacture our products in the UK at our Sheffield facility and deliver them directly to you — from schools and churches to theatres and event venues nationwide. Call 0114 399 2978 or email enquiries@portablestage.co.uk and we will arrange delivery for your stage.
